**Version 1.0 - Effective 2026**

This document describes the trademark policy for the **Endue Citizenry**

the Apache License 2.0 (see `LICENSE`), which is a generous, permissive
license. However, the Apache License does **not** grant permission to use
the trade names, trademarks, service marks, or product names of the
Licensor (Apache License 2.0, Section 6).

This policy clarifies how the Endue and Endue Citizenry marks may and
may not be used.

---

## 1. Marks Covered

The following are trademarks of **Endue** (collectively, the "Marks"):

- **Endue**
- **Endue Citizenry**
- **Citizenry** (when used in connection with identity, authentication,
  authorization, secrets management, or related infrastructure software
  or services)
- The Endue and Citizenry logos, visual identity, and any future marks
  formally designated by Endue as part of the Project

The Marks are protected regardless of whether they are formally
registered in a given jurisdiction. Trademark rights arise from use, and
Endue actively uses the Marks in connection with the Project.

## 2. Permitted Uses (No Permission Required)

You may use the Marks **without seeking permission** for the following
purposes:

### 2.1 Accurate factual reference

You may use the Marks to truthfully describe the Project, including:

- Stating that your software is built on, integrates with, is compatible
  with, or supports Endue Citizenry.
- Examples: *"Powered by Endue Citizenry"*, *"Compatible with Endue
  Citizenry Protocol v1.x"*, *"Built on Endue Citizenry"*, *"Supports
  Endue Citizenry"*.

### 2.2 Editorial and educational use

- News articles, blog posts, books, conference talks, tutorials, and
  academic papers that reference the Project.
- Comparative reviews and benchmarks.

### 2.3 Distribution of unmodified source

- Distributing unmodified copies of the Project's source code or
  official binary releases under the Apache License 2.0, retaining the
  `LICENSE`, `NOTICE`, and trademark notices.

### 2.4 Source-level identification within modifications

- Retaining the Marks in source code comments, copyright headers,
  package metadata, and `NOTICE` files when distributing modified
  versions, as required by the Apache License 2.0.

## 3. Restricted Uses (Permission Required)

The following uses require **prior written permission** from Endue.
Contact: `team@endue.ai`.

### 3.1 Product, company, or service names

You may **not** use any of the Marks as, or as part of, the name of your
own product, company, organization, service, or domain name. This
includes (non-exhaustive list):

- Using "Citizenry" or "Endue" as part of a product brand
  (e.g., "AcmeCitizenry", "Citizenry Pro by Acme", "Endue Cloud by X").
- Using the Marks in a service offering name
  (e.g., "Hosted Citizenry by Provider X", "Managed Endue Citizenry").
- Registering domain names that incorporate the Marks
  (e.g., `citizenry-cloud.com`, `endue-hosting.io`,
  `provider-citizenry.dev`).
- Using the Marks in mobile app names, package registry names
  (npm, PyPI, crates.io, etc.) without coordination with Endue.

This restriction applies regardless of whether the underlying software
is a fork of the Project. **A fork is not entitled to use the Marks.**
If you fork the Project, you must rename the fork to a name that does
not incorporate the Marks.

### 3.2 Conformance, certification, and compatibility claims

You may **not** claim that your implementation is "Endue Citizenry
Certified", "Citizenry-Certified", "Official Citizenry", or any
substantially similar designation unless your implementation has passed
the official Endue Citizenry Conformance Test Suite and you have
entered into a conformance mark agreement with Endue.

Use of certification or conformance marks ("Certified Endue Citizenry",
the conformance badge, etc.) requires participation in the Endue
Citizenry Conformance Program. See `GOVERNANCE.md` for details.

You **may** make truthful compatibility statements such as *"Compatible
with Endue Citizenry Protocol v1.x"* without entering the conformance
program, provided the statement is accurate and not misleading.

### 3.3 Logos, visual identity, and merchandise

Reproduction or modification of the Endue or Citizenry logos requires
prior written permission. This includes use on websites, products,
merchandise (T-shirts, stickers, conference swag), and printed
materials, except for editorial or factual reference described in
Section 2.

### 3.4 Modified distributions presented as "Endue Citizenry"

You may **not** distribute a modified version of the Project under the
Endue Citizenry name in a way that could mislead users into believing
the modified version is the official Project. If you distribute a
modified version, you must clearly indicate that it is a modified
version and not the official Project, and you must not use the Marks in
a way that implies official endorsement.

### 3.5 Implication of official endorsement, partnership, or sponsorship

You may **not** use the Marks in any manner that implies endorsement,
sponsorship, certification, partnership, or other official relationship
with Endue unless such a relationship actually exists and has been
documented in writing.

## 4. Specific Rules for Public Cloud and Hosting Providers

Endue welcomes public cloud providers, hosting providers, managed
service providers, and platform vendors to deploy and offer the Project.
We have designed this policy to make such adoption easy while preserving
the integrity of the Marks.

### 4.1 What you may do

- Offer hosted, managed, or platform-integrated versions of the Project.
- Describe the service truthfully (e.g., *"Acme Identity Service -
  powered by Endue Citizenry"*, *"Compatible with Endue Citizenry"*).
- Reference the Project in marketing materials, documentation, and
  technical descriptions.
- Use the Endue Citizenry name in factual, descriptive ways to inform
  customers what software they are running.

### 4.2 What you may not do

- Name your service using the Marks (e.g., *"AWS Citizenry"*, *"Azure
  Endue"*, *"Google Cloud Citizenry"*, *"Cloudflare Endue"*).
- Imply that your hosted offering is the official, original, or
  endorsed version of the Project unless a partnership agreement exists.
- Use the Marks more prominently than your own brand in service
  identity, such that customers could reasonably believe Endue is the
  service provider.

### 4.3 Partnership program

Endue offers a formal partnership program for hosting providers that
wish to obtain expanded mark usage rights, official partner badges, and
conformance certification. Contact `team@endue.ai`.

## 5. Conformance Mark and Program

Endue operates the Endue Citizenry Conformance Program. Implementations
that pass the official conformance test suite may apply to use the
"Endue Citizenry Certified" mark under a separate conformance agreement.

- The conformance test suite is open source (Apache 2.0).
- Certification is open to all implementers, including commercial
  competitors and public cloud providers.
- The conformance mark is a separate trademark from the underlying Marks
  and is subject to its own usage agreement.

See `GOVERNANCE.md` for the conformance program details.

## 6. Enforcement

Endue reserves all rights in the Marks. This policy is provided to
clarify acceptable uses; it does not waive Endue's right to enforce its
trademark rights in cases of misuse, dilution, confusion, or
infringement.

If you become aware of a use of the Marks that you believe is
inappropriate, please contact `team@endue.ai`.

If you have used the Marks in a way that conflicts with this policy,
please remedy the use (e.g., rename your product, remove confusing
references) within 30 days of notice from Endue. Most issues are
resolved cooperatively without legal action.
